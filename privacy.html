Offline play

Arcane Chess does not require an account and contains no advertising, analytics SDK or in-app purchases. Offline game history, settings and camera preferences are stored on your device. The developer does not receive these saves. The Android app disables system backup of its app data.

Optional online games

Online play starts only when you create or join an invitation. The app uses PeerJS Cloud (0.peerjs.com) for connection signaling and Google STUN servers to help devices connect. These services necessarily receive network information such as your IP address and connection/session identifiers. Signaling also exchanges connection descriptions and invitation-related peer identifiers. Your opponent can receive network information needed for the direct peer connection, potentially including your IP address.

Chess moves, board position, game history, readiness and connection status, and a randomly generated session token are exchanged with the other player over an encrypted WebRTC data channel. No microphone, camera, contact list, location sensor, chat or voice recording is used. Online play has no paid relay or developer-operated game database. Internet connectivity and successful peer connections are not guaranteed.

Purpose and retention

Online data is used to establish the game, validate moves and recover brief interruptions. Invitation/session identifiers and online game state are kept in the running app's memory. Closing or reloading the app ends that session; previous offline saves remain. Network service operators may maintain their own operational logs under their own policies; the developer does not control their retention and does not promise that those services retain nothing.

Third-party information: PeerJS · Google Privacy Policy. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which has its own privacy statement.

Your choices and deletion

Use solo or same-device play to avoid the optional online connection services. Leave an online room or close the app to end the session. Clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall it to remove local saves and preferences. No in-app account exists to delete. For privacy questions or requests concerning information you sent directly to the developer, contact the email above. Support emails contain the information you choose to send and are used to answer your request. Do not send passwords or sensitive personal information in bug reports.

Children and changes

The intended audience is age 13 and above; the game is not designed specifically for children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information. Contact us if you believe a child has sent personal information directly to us. Changes to app data practices will be reflected in an updated policy.

İsteğe bağlı çevrimiçi oyun

Çevrimiçi bağlantı yalnızca davet oluşturduğunuzda veya bir davete katıldığınızda başlar. Bağlantı kurulurken PeerJS Cloud (0.peerjs.com) ve Google STUN sunucuları kullanılır. Bu hizmetler IP adresi, bağlantı ve oturum kimlikleri gibi ağ bilgilerini alır. Sinyalleşme sırasında bağlantı açıklamaları ve davetle ilişkili eş kimlikleri iletilir. Doğrudan bağlantı nedeniyle rakibiniz, IP adresiniz dahil bağlantı için gereken ağ bilgilerini alabilir.

Hamleler, tahta konumu, maç geçmişi, hazır olma ve bağlantı durumu ile rastgele üretilen oturum belirteci diğer oyuncuyla şifreli WebRTC veri kanalı üzerinden paylaşılır. Mikrofon, kamera, rehber, konum sensörü, sohbet ve ses kaydı kullanılmaz. Ücretli aktarım sunucusu ve geliştiricinin işlettiği bir oyun veritabanı yoktur. Bazı ağlarda bağlantı kurulamayabilir.
