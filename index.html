Arcane Chess

Cinematic fantasy chess by oziverson93.

Play against Stockfish, share a device, or invite a friend for a private online duel. Android 9+, WebGL 2 and a current Android System WebView are required. The interface is currently in English. Online invitations are a beta feature and may not connect on every network.
